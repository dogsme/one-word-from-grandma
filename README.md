One word: the word Grandma would text about AI.

## The word

**contraption**

Grandma does not say "artificial intelligence". She looks at the talking
box on the counter, the thing that answers back and knows her shopping list,
and she calls it what she has always called any clever machine she does not
quite trust: a contraption. Warm, a little baffled, and funny all on its own —
no caption needed.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes exactly that one word, and nothing else, to
`/output/entry.txt` as UTF-8, with no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
